Chapter 1 — Introduction

🌊 The Problem

  • India faces 30+ major disasters annually
  • Manual shelter allocation leads to overcrowding, delays & chaos
  • No unified real-time system for shelter capacity tracking
  • Resource misallocation costs lives during golden hour

🎯 Our Solution

  • AI-powered shelter matching with multi-criteria optimization
  • Real-time NDMA/TNSDMA/IMD disaster telemetry
  • Multi-agent coordination for emergency response
  • Intelligent chatbot for victim assistance

💡 Key Innovation

RESCUE AI combines OR-Tools constraint optimization, multi-agent AI coordination, and real-time geospatial intelligence into a unified full-stack platform for disaster emergency response.

Chapter 4 — Methodology

🧮 Allocation Algorithm

  • Input: Victim profile (location, family size, medical needs, vulnerability)
  • Constraints: Shelter capacity, distance, accessibility, disaster type
  • Solver: Google OR-Tools CP-SAT with weighted objectives
  • Output: Optimal shelter assignment with priority score

🤖 Multi-Agent System

  • Triage Agent: Classifies victim urgency (P1–P4)
  • Logistics Agent: Routes transport & supplies
  • Medical Agent: Coordinates healthcare resources
  • Communication Agent: Manages alerts & notifications
